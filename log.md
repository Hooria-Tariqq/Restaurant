Hey there! I am not sure if anyone is ever gonna visit my GitHub or scroll through my repos. BUT THIS IS FOR MY FUTURE SELF (angoor to khatty hain!!!).
I built this app to solve a very specific problem that me and my friends face every single weekend: we physically cannot decide where or what to eat. 
It's an absolute struggle. The more people in the group, the more harder it gets to choose. Someone blocks the spot I suggested, someone else has zero opinions until you pick a place (DUFFERRR!!), and suddenly we've been sitting in the corrifor for 45 minutes debating burgers vs. biryani
Look, I know it’s not the end of the world, but I am literally getting an AI degree—why shouldn't I put it to work solving the problem in my daily life?
So, I built this app to randomly pick a restaurant for us. I’ll keep adding cooler features as I go, but for now: IT PICKS THE SPOT, AND NOBODY GETS A SAY. NOT EVEN ME. Whatever the algorithm outputs, we are legally bound to go there. 
Let's be real—whenever we get stuck, we always end up defaulting to our national spots Kababjees or Allah Wala Biryani anyway. So instead of having that whole 45-minute argument just to end up there, the app takes the wheel, forces a choice from our curated Also, why not take full advantage of having a university right in the heart of a working street food haven like Hussainabad? Honestly, writing this out made me realize I should probably start building more mini-apps for all the random things we can't decide on during our free time.
